# Competitors

Checked 20 November 2026. Prices are a month. "Not published" means the company doesn't show a price.

Of the 255 kept dealers in [prospects.md](prospects.md), CarsIreland runs the sites of about 90, HappyDealer 54, DoneDeal 18 and manufacturer templates about 15 (Toyota 9, Ford, Opel, VW, BYD). 29 have no site. NetDirector, mtc and WordPress agencies have a few each.

Every rival sells a site. None of the site providers checked shows a leads inbox with follow-ups, WhatsApp alerts to staff, review replies and margins together. Sell that, with the site included.

## CarsIreland dealer sites
**What dealers get:** a "Powered by CarsIreland" site with stock search, finance calculator and apply, Google reviews, test drive booking, WhatsApp on each car, and reserve or buy online ([jamesbarrymotors.ie](https://www.jamesbarrymotors.ie/), checked 2026-11-20).
**Price:** not published. A forum figure of about €300 is in [pricing-strategy.md](pricing-strategy.md).
**Weak for the dealer:**
- The "Get a Valuation" form asks only for a name and phone, no reg lookup, and its description is placeholder text ([jamesbarrymotors.ie](https://www.jamesbarrymotors.ie/), 2026-11-20).
- On a phone, a chat box covers the car search and a cookie banner covers the rest ([card](cards/limerick.md), 2026-11-20).
- Sites show a handful of reviews: Kenny Galway shows three, the newest nine months old, against 353 on Google ([card](cards/galway.md), 2026-11-20).
- CarsIreland says dealers "are notified as soon as an enquiry is made" ([Trustpilot reply](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/carsireland.ie), 2026-11-20); no dealer dashboard is mentioned.

**Line:** "Your CarsIreland site gets the lead. Who picks it up, and how do you know they did?"

## HappyDealer
**What dealers get:** a custom-designed site with a control panel, stock management, a CRM and service scheduling. They also sell SEO, PPC, social and content ([happydealer.ie/web-development](https://www.happydealer.ie/web-development), [happydealer.ie](https://www.happydealer.ie/), 2026-11-20).
**Price:** not published.
**Weak for the dealer:**
- Frank Hogan's site (839 Google reviews) shows one review on the home page and has no trade-in valuation: car pages offer Enquire, Finance, Reserve and Test Drive, and nothing to value the buyer's own car ([frankhogan.ie](https://www.frankhogan.ie/), 2026-11-20).
- AutoXpress's home page downloads 34 MB on a phone before a buyer can search ([card](cards/limerick.md), 2026-11-20).
- A typo ("FINAINCE") in an offer on motorview.ie, and a home-page review with a name but no words on Mallow Road Motors ([cards/cork.md](cards/cork.md), 2026-11-20).
- Its CRM is one line on its site, with no detail on what it does ([happydealer.ie/web-development](https://www.happydealer.ie/web-development)).

**Line:** "HappyDealer built you a good-looking site. Can a buyer value their trade-in on it, and can you see which leads got a call back?"

## DoneDeal Pro Dealer Websites and Dealer Hub
**What dealers get:**
- A free branded site, stock synced from DoneDeal, finance applications through lenders including Bank of Ireland, Close Brothers and LoanITT. Square1 built the platform; DoneDeal's Pro Success team sets up each dealer's site ([Square1 case study](https://www.square1.io/work/donedeal-dealer-websites), 2026-11-20).
- Dealer Hub for stock, Price Assist, card deposits through Stripe, SmartReach retargeting and SmartReach Social for Facebook and Instagram. WhatsApp leads, a branded showroom and Trusted Dealer only on the Accelerator and Ultimate packages ([sales.donedeal.ie/motors](https://www.sales.donedeal.ie/motors), 2026-11-20).

**Price:** the site is free: "Hosted by DoneDeal at no extra cost to the dealer" ([Square1](https://www.square1.io/work/donedeal-dealer-websites)), "your own FREE Dealer Website" ([sales.donedeal.ie/motors](https://www.sales.donedeal.ie/motors)). Subscriptions are not published; forum posts put them at €130 + VAT to €500 a month ([boards.ie](https://www.boards.ie/discussion/2058380932/why-do-dealers-stick-with-carzone-and-carsireland-despite-high-fees), about two years old).
**Weak for the dealer:**
- One shared platform behind hundreds of dealer sites, built from the same content blocks ([Square1](https://www.square1.io/work/donedeal-dealer-websites)), with a "Powered by DoneDeal" link back to the marketplace in the footer ([dcautolinekerry.ie](https://www.dcautolinekerry.ie/), 2026-11-20).
- DC Autoline's site has no Google reviews, no test drive booking and no trade-in valuation: its Trade-In page is a plain form (make, model, year, mileage) with no reg lookup ([dcautolinekerry.ie/trade-in](https://www.dcautolinekerry.ie/trade-in), 2026-11-20).
- DoneDeal hosts the site, so it likely goes if the dealer stops paying for DoneDeal. Ask the dealer.

**Line:** "Your free site has DoneDeal's name on it and links buyers back to a page full of other dealers' cars. Keep your DoneDeal ads; we put the same stock on a site with only your name on it."

## Manufacturer templates (Toyota Ireland as the example)
**What dealers get:** new-car range with prices and offers, used search, finance calculator and quotes, trade-in request, service and test drive booking, leasing, owner pages, run by Toyota Ireland ([grandons.ie](https://www.grandons.ie/), 2026-11-20).
**Price:** not published; part of the franchise.
**Weak for the dealer:**
- No reviews and no WhatsApp or chat: Grandons has 998 Google reviews at 4.7 and none on the site ([grandons.ie](https://www.grandons.ie/), 2026-11-20).
- The dealer doesn't control it: O'Callaghan Motors' "Explore" link goes to a Toyota dealer in Tallaght ([card](cards/cork.md)); Divane VW's site showed "Down for maintenance" all day ([card](cards/kerry.md)); Reen's site still knows only Peugeot after two years of Fiat and Jeep ([card](cards/kerry.md)).
- Opel's template at John Kelly still has a COVID mask page ([card](cards/waterford.md)).

**Line:** "Toyota runs your new-car site and that stays. We give you what it doesn't: your used cars, your reviews, and one inbox for every lead from that site."

## Others that matter
- **NetDirector (GForces; footers now link to Keyloop) and mtc (UK, with a Cork office):** dealer website platforms for groups (Lyons, Al Hayes, Kerry Motor Works, Noel Deasy); NetDirector is billed as "Automotive Ecommerce". Price not published ([kerrymotorworks.ie](https://www.kerrymotorworks.ie/), [mtc](https://www.mtc.co.uk/blog/launching-a-next-gen-koda-dealership-website-with-noel-deasy-cars), 2026-11-20). Strong sites; lead with the dashboard and sell Dealer with ours as their used-car site. Line: "Your site does almost everything. Show me where your leads, reviews and margins live."
- **MotorDesk:** dealer software plus a site, €89, €189 and €310 a month ([Sage Marketplace](https://ie-marketplace.sage.com/en-IE/apps/116385/motordesk), 2026-11-20). UK-built ([motordesk.com](https://www.motordesk.com/): "Developed in-house in the UK"); no sign of DoneDeal or CarsIreland stock sync on that listing. The cheapest real alternative to Dealer.
- **AutoFeed:** Irish, builds dealer sites and a WordPress plugin fed live from DoneDeal Dealer Hub, with reg lookups through Cartell. No CRM mentioned; price not published ([autofeed.dev](https://autofeed.dev/), 2026-11-20). Closest to our Showroom tier.
- **Eskimo:** UK CRM with DoneDeal, CarsIreland, Carzone and WhatsApp integrations, used by Nissan Ireland ([autoindustrycompare.com](https://www.autoindustrycompare.com/providers_crm.php), 2026-11-20). The closest rival to our dashboard; not checked in depth.

## Where Checkwork wins
- After the lead: the site's enquiries in one inbox, follow-ups, staff roles, WhatsApp alerts. No site provider above shows this on its public pages; HappyDealer's CRM is unverified.
- Reviews: the Google rating, the count and up to five reviews as Google shows them, with a link to every review, plus replies from the dashboard. Most rivals show none or a few.
- Trade-in with reg lookup: missing or a plain form on the CarsIreland, HappyDealer, DoneDeal and Toyota sites checked.
- The site carries the dealer's name only, on their own domain, with stock synced from the DoneDeal or CarsIreland account they already pay for, and on Dealer and Group their data exports any time.
- Margins, stock age, and AI descriptions copied back to DoneDeal: none of the site providers checked show these.

## Where it doesn't
- Price against free: DoneDeal's site costs nothing extra. Showroom alone (€249) is a hard sale to a DoneDeal dealer; sell Dealer.
- New cars on franchises: we don't replace Toyota's, Ford's or VW's site. We sit beside it as the used-car site and the inbox.
- Marketplace reach: DoneDeal claims 7m visits a month ([sales.donedeal.ie](https://sales.donedeal.ie), 2026-11-20). We don't replace the listings, so we're a second bill and must pay for ourselves in leads.
- Already built in by rivals: online deposits and Stripe payments (DoneDeal, CarsIreland), finance applications to named lenders (DoneDeal), Facebook retargeting (DoneDeal SmartReach Social), service booking (Toyota, HappyDealer; ours is the €49 add-on).
- Big groups on NetDirector or mtc: strong sites and long contracts. Sell Dealer or Group with ours as their used-car site; a slower sale.

## Gaps worth building
1. Finance applications to a real lender, as well as the estimator. DoneDeal already sends applications to Bank of Ireland and Close Brothers.
2. Facebook and Instagram leads into the inbox (already a price-rise trigger). Dealers will compare it with DoneDeal's SmartReach Social.
3. One inbox that also takes DoneDeal and CarsIreland enquiries and phone enquiries, so the dashboard is worth it even to a dealer who keeps a free DoneDeal site.
4. One inbox, reports per branch or brand, and several Google listings for groups with separate sites, plus syncing several DoneDeal accounts.
5. A dashboard-only plan for franchise and NetDirector dealers who won't change site.
