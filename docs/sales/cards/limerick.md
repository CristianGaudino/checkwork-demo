# Sales cards: County Limerick

8 tier A and 13 tier B dealers (21 cards). Site facts as of 20 Nov 2026.
Strongest three: **AutoXpress** (332 cars worth about €8m, a homepage that downloads 34 MB on a phone, no trade-in form, newest review shown is from December 2025), **Frank Hogan** (303 cars over three DoneDeal accounts, 839 reviews with one shown, no trade-in form), **Lyons of Limerick** (160 cars, 475 reviews not shown, "Valuations" button goes to the contact page).
Most big Limerick franchise dealers already have decent sites (CarsIreland, HappyDealer, NetDirector, Toyota's template), so lead with the dashboard, reviews and trade-in more than with "your site is broken". Best plain website sale: **John Culhane Car Sales** (104 cars, the domain on its DoneDeal page does not exist).

## Tier A

### Lyons of Limerick, Limerick city (Ballysimon Road) · Tier A, 83/100 (able 50, willing 18, opening 15)
**Who they are:** Ford, Volvo, Jaguar, Land Rover and BYD franchise; second branch at Briar Hill, Galway (see `galway.json`). Calls itself "Munster's largest automotive group". 160 cars on DoneDeal, typical price €24,000, about €3.8m of stock. 475 Google reviews at 4.2. Site on NetDirector (Keyloop), a paid dealer platform with "Search and Buy Online".
**What we found:**
- No reviews anywhere on the homepage, despite 475 Google reviews (https://www.lyonsoflimerick.com/).
- The "Valuations" button goes to the general contact page (/contact-us/lyons-of-limerick-group/): there is no trade-in form.
- Rough edges a buyer sees: the breadcrumb reads "Homepage > Homepage", and the LinkedIn link in the footer is broken (linkedin.com/in/in/...).
- On a phone, the cookie banner fills the bottom half of the first screen.
**Sales points:**
1. "475 people reviewed you on Google and your website doesn't show one." Reviews on the site, replies from the dashboard (Reviews).
2. "Your Valuations button sends buyers to a contact form. Let them value their car there and then." Trade-in with photos and reg lookup (Trade-in and sell your car, Registration lookup on the website).
3. "Two branches, two counties, one list of leads." Leads inbox, follow-ups and WhatsApp alerts for Limerick and Galway (Dealer dashboard, Sales and customers, WhatsApp).
4. "Five brands of new cars plus used: what is actually making you money?" (Margins and reports, New cars and factory orders).
**Pitch:** Group at €898 a month (€749 plus €149 for Galway), with our site for the used side; New cars for all five brands adds €87 (3 x €29), €985 a month. €3.8m of stock, two branches and a paid platform: they spend, and the gaps (reviews, trade-in, follow-up) are dashboard features.
**Opener:** "You have 475 Google reviews and none of them are on lyonsoflimerick.com, and your Valuations button just opens a contact form; we fix both and give you one dashboard for Limerick and Galway."
**Caveats:** Ford, Volvo and JLR all have rules on dealer sites and NetDirector may be the approved supplier: lead with used cars and the dashboard, not replacing the site. Group decision, likely made centrally for both branches.
