# Sales cards: County Galway

10 tier A and 19 tier B dealers (29 cards). Site facts as of 20 Nov 2026.
Strongest three: **Al Hayes Motors** (458 cars, a homepage offer that links to a file on a staff PC, no reviews shown), **Colm Quinn BMW Galway** (165 cars worth about €9m, 481 reviews not shown, no finance calculator or trade-in form), **Kenny Galway** (139 cars, Galway Races sponsor, a 4.0 rating and the same three old reviews on its site).
Most big Galway franchise dealers already have decent sites (CarsIreland, HappyDealer, manufacturer templates), so lead with the dashboard, reviews and follow-ups more than with "your site is broken".

## Tier A

### Colm Quinn BMW Galway, Galway City (Tuam Road) · Tier A, 83/100 (able 50, willing 18, opening 15)
**Who they are:** BMW main dealer (DoneDeal also lists MINI), one of three Colm Quinn BMW branches (Galway, Athlone, Drogheda), each with its own site. 165 cars on cars.ie, typical price €54,000, so about €8.9m of stock. 481 Google reviews at 4.6. **What we found:**
- No reviews anywhere on the home page, despite 481 Google reviews at 4.6 (https://www.colmquinnbmwgalway.ie/).
- Finance is BMW's offers pages only ("BMW Finance Offers"); no payment estimator on their own cars (https://www.colmquinnbmwgalway.ie/finance-offers/bmw/).
- The only trade-in on the site is BMW's EV trade-in offer article; there is no form to value a buyer's car (https://www.colmquinnbmwgalway.ie/news-and-offers/bmw/the-bmw-ev-trade-in-offer).

**Sales points:**
1. "481 people gave you 4.6 stars and none of them are on your website." Reviews on the site, replies from the dashboard (Reviews).
2. "A BMW buyer wants to know the monthly figure on the car they are looking at, not a brand offer." Finance estimator on every used car (Finance).
3. "Every buyer has a car to trade. Let them value it before they ring." Trade-in form with photos and reg lookup (Trade-in, Registration lookup on the website).
4. "Three branches, three sites, one inbox." Dashboard with leads inbox, follow-ups and staff logins (Dealer dashboard, WhatsApp).
**Pitch:** Group at €1,047 a month (€749 plus €149 each for Athlone and Drogheda), with our site for the used side. €9m of stock in Galway alone and three Carzone sites: they spend, and the dashboard is where the group value is.
**Opener:** "You have 481 Google reviews at 4.6 and not one of them is on colmquinnbmwgalway.ie; we put them on the site and let you reply from one dashboard for all three branches."
**Caveats:** BMW controls new-car pages and service booking; lead with used cars and the dashboard. Group decision (Colm Quinn runs Athlone and Drogheda too).
