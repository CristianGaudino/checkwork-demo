# Objections

What dealers will say, what they usually mean, and what to say back. Prices are a month, before VAT ([pricing-strategy.md](pricing-strategy.md)). Show, don't argue: every answer ends on demo.checkwork.ie or a question.

## Ground rules
- Agree first, then answer with their own numbers from the card.
- Never discount. The only offers are in the pricing doc: a year upfront gets a month free; a 2-year contract waives setup.
- Only promise what's built: one site per dealer, with one leads inbox for its enquiries and reviews from one Google listing. Not built yet: one inbox across separate sites, per-branch or per-brand reports, several Google listings, leads from outside the site (manufacturer sites, chat tools, Facebook, DoneDeal or CarsIreland messages, phone enquiries), syncing several DoneDeal or CarsIreland accounts, reserving or paying a deposit online. Don't promise those, or discounts.
- Shown in the demo but not yet connected for a real dealer: the DoneDeal or CarsIreland stock sync, the reg lookup, sending WhatsApp messages, posting to Facebook and Instagram, running ads, newsletters, and review replies to Google. Say they're coming, and don't give a launch date that depends on them.

## "We already have a website" (HappyDealer, CarsIreland, DoneDeal template)
**Means:** it works well enough and switching sounds like hassle.
**Say:** "Fair enough, and we don't touch your listings: we'll pull your stock from DoneDeal or CarsIreland, so nothing changes for whoever lists the cars. What you get that the template doesn't is your Google reviews on the site, a trade-in form with a reg lookup, and every lead from the site in one inbox with follow-ups."
**Proof:** their card's opener (reviews missing, no trade-in, a broken link). Open their site and the demo side by side on a phone.
**Ask:** "When a lead comes in from your site today, how do you know someone rang them back?"

## "It's more than we pay now"
**Means:** they see a second bill, not more sales.
**Say:** "It is a second bill: your listings stay. So it has to pay for itself. Dealer is €449 a month. At your typical price of €[card figure], one extra sale or one trade-in you'd have missed covers months of it."
**Proof:** site analytics show which channels and tagged ads bring leads to the site. Showroom at €249 if they only want the site.
**Ask:** "How many enquiries a month do you think go unanswered or get answered late?"
**Don't:** cut the price. Offer the year upfront (a month free) if cash flow is the worry.

## "The manufacturer controls our site"
**Means:** they can't drop it, and may not pay for it.
**Say:** "Keep it for new cars. We'd be your used-car site next to it, synced from your DoneDeal or CarsIreland stock. The dashboard gives you every lead from our site in one inbox, your reviews on show, and stock age, and margins with the €49 add-on."
**Proof:** cards for Toyota, Opel, VW, Kia and Ford dealers all lead this way. Never suggest replacing the manufacturer's site.
**Ask:** "Who handles your used-car enquiries today, and where do they land?"

## "We're too small"
**Means:** they don't want to pay for things they won't use.
**Say:** "Showroom is built for 30 to 70 cars: your own site, your stock synced, reviews, finance estimator, trade-in and WhatsApp click to chat, for €249. No dashboard to learn."
**Proof:** they can move up to Dealer later and the setup they paid comes off the new tier's setup.
**Ask:** "If the site brought you one more trade-in a month, would that be worth it?"
(Under about 25 cars: thank them and move on.)

## "We get our leads from DoneDeal anyway"
**Means:** DoneDeal works, why add anything.
**Say:** "Keep it, we'll sync from it. But on DoneDeal your car sits beside your competitors'. Buyers who search your name, or come from your reviews, should land on a page with only your cars, a finance figure and a trade-in valuation."
**Proof:** the demo's car page vs their DoneDeal ad. Dealer's AI improves a car's description, ready to paste into DoneDeal.
**Ask:** "How many people Google your name each week? Where do they end up?"

## "What's the setup fee for?"
**Means:** paying before seeing anything.
**Say:** "It pays for the 8 to 12 hours of building your site: syncing your stock, your brand, your Google profile and going live. It's €950 on Showroom and Dealer. On a 2-year contract it's waived."
**Ask:** "Would you rather pay it once or spread the commitment over two years?"
**Don't:** waive it on a monthly plan.

## "I don't want to be locked into 2 years"
**Means:** they've been burned by a long contract.
**Say:** "You don't have to be. Monthly is the standard: pay month to month at the list price. The 2-year option only exists to waive the setup fee. Dropping a module takes effect from the 1st of the next month."
**Ask:** "Which matters more to you: no setup fee, or the freedom to stop?"

## "We tried an agency before"
**Means:** paid for a site that went stale, or never heard back.
**Say:** "What went wrong? [Listen.] This isn't a one-off build. Your stock will sync itself from DoneDeal or CarsIreland, your reviews come straight from Google, and you change cars and staff from the dashboard yourself."
**Proof:** walk through the demo dashboard: add a car, reply to a review, assign a lead.
**Ask:** "If you could change one thing on your site today without ringing anyone, what would it be?"

## "What if you go out of business? You're one person."
**Means:** a real risk, and they're right to ask.
**Say:** "Fair question. You're never trapped: it's monthly, your stock stays on DoneDeal or CarsIreland, and on Dealer and Group, owners can export leads, customers and stock to a spreadsheet any time. I'm also deliberately taking on only as many dealers as I can look after properly."
**Ask:** "What would you need to see to feel comfortable?"
**Don't:** claim a team or support hours you don't have.

## "We don't need AI"
**Means:** sounds like a gimmick, or a cost.
**Say:** "You don't have to use it. It drafts replies to leads and reviews and tidies car descriptions; you edit or ignore them. Showroom has no AI at all. On Dealer, 100 uses a month are included and extra use stops at €50 unless you agree otherwise; you can set it lower on the Billing page."
**Ask:** "Who writes your replies to Google reviews now?"

## "Head office decides" (groups)
**Means:** you're talking to the wrong person, or they want a reason to stop.
**Say:** "Makes sense. Can we get them on a 20-minute call with the demo? I'll send a one-page summary first."
**Proof:** Group is €749 plus €149 per extra branch, setup €1,950: margins and reports, social, newsletter, new cars and orders for 2 brands, lookups and WhatsApp alerts for every branch. Don't promise per-branch reports or one inbox across separate sites.
**Ask:** "Who at head office looks at leads and margins? What do they use now?"
Expect a longer sale. Note the date and follow up.

## "What about data protection / our customers' data?"
**Means:** GDPR worry, or they've been asked before.
**Say:** "On Dealer and Group, when someone asks what you hold on them, or to be deleted, the dashboard finds everything by their email or phone: forms, leads, test drives, purchases. You download a copy or erase it in a few clicks. Leads are kept 12 months then deleted, purchases lose the buyer's details after six years, and the site's privacy page says so. On Showroom, we do it for you."
**Proof:** Data requests in the demo dashboard; every export of personal data is logged.
**Ask:** "How would you answer a data request today?"

## "Let me think about it"
**Means:** one of the above, not said yet.
**Say:** "Of course. What's the one thing that would make it a yes?" Then use the matching answer. Book a follow-up date before you hang up.
