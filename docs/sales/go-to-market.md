# Go to market

One person sells, onboards and supports. Plan by hours: about 10 hours a week selling, the rest onboarding (8 to 12 hours a dealer) and support. That makes 2 signed dealers a month the pace, and about 20 live dealers the ceiling before help is needed (see [pricing-strategy.md](pricing-strategy.md)).

## What we sell
The three tiers only, at list price: Showroom €249, Dealer €449, Group €749 plus €149 per extra branch, before VAT. No launch discount. The only levers are the written terms: a year upfront gets a month free, a 2-year contract waives setup.

- Push Dealer: most of the first 20 should be on it.
- Franchise dealers on a manufacturer site get Dealer, with our site as their used-car site next to the manufacturer's. Never pitch replacing the manufacturer site.
- Groups: sell Group (€749 plus €149 per extra branch), led by reviews, trade-in and the dashboard. Each site has its own leads inbox for its enquiries and its reviews from one Google listing. One inbox across separate sites waits for #226: a group with several sites moves onto one of ours.
- At setup we connect the dealer's own accounts to their site: DoneDeal or CarsIreland for the stock, WhatsApp, their Google profile, and Facebook, Instagram, ads and email for the add-ons they buy. The demo shows all of it working.

## Timing
- Now to 19 December: dealers are quieter before the 271 plate. Best weeks of the year to get in the door.
- 5 to 31 January: forecourts are flat out. Book visits for February; only follow up quotes already sent.
- July is the same (272 plate). Sell hard in May, June, September to November.

## Channel and sequence
Visits close car dealers. Calls book visits. Email only confirms what was said.

1. Call the dealership's published landline. Ask who decides on the website, and for 20 minutes on a weekday morning. Use the card's opener in one line.
2. Visit with a phone and a laptop. Show the problem from the card on their own site, on a phone. Then show demo.checkwork.ie in the Dealer view: reviews, trade-in with reg lookup, the leads inbox, WhatsApp alerts. Name the tier and the price in the room.
3. Quote within 48 hours: one page with tier, add-ons from the card, setup, and the three payment options.
4. Follow up by phone on day 3 and day 10. After that, monthly, unless they've said no.
5. Once signed, get the order form, first invoice (setup or the year upfront), DoneDeal or CarsIreland account, logo and Google profile access. Book the launch date on the day they sign.

Ask for the 2-year contract when setup is the objection. Lead with monthly plus setup: the setup fee pays for onboarding time.

## Staying within the rules
The ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. 336 of 2011, Regulation 13) and GDPR apply; the Data Protection Commission explains them on dataprotection.ie. Sole traders and partnerships count as individuals. Each breach is a separate offence, so these are rules for every call and email.

- Email to a limited company's business address is allowed without consent. Check on core.cro.ie that the business is a company (Ltd, DAC, plc or UC), not a business name registered to a person. We email a sole trader or partnership only after they agree on the call (stricter than the law requires), so call or visit instead. Every email names Checkwork, gives a postal address and a working opt-out, and an opt-out is honoured at once.
- Calls go to the published business landline. Before the first call, check the number isn't on the National Directory Database opt-out (marketers get access through the NDD's operator; see comreg.ie), and never call anyone who has said no. Never cold call a mobile number (083 to 089), whoever it belongs to, a company's included, unless they've told us they're happy to get calls on it.
- Treat SMS and WhatsApp like email: no cold messages.
- Visits have no consent rule, but leave if asked and don't come back.
- Keep a do-not-contact list in the tracker. A named contact goes in only as published on the dealer's site or given to us. On first contact with a named person (always, for a sole trader or partnership), say who we are, where we got their details and that they can tell us to stop at any time, and point them to the privacy notice on checkwork.ie.

## Weekly targets
| Each week | Target |
|---|---|
| Calls (new dealers) | 10 |
| Visits and demos | 4 |
| Quotes sent | 2 |
| Signed | 1 every 2 weeks |
| Selling hours | 10, no more while 2 or more dealers are onboarding |

Track every dealer in one sheet: called, visited, quoted, signed, live, lost (and why), do not contact (date), and email OK (date, who said yes).

## Proof
- The first 3 live dealers: ask for one quote and permission to name them. Until then, show only the demo; never invent a reference.
- After 30 days live, show each dealer their leads from the site. That number is the case for the next sale.

## Numbers that decide
| Measure | Working | Change something |
|---|---|---|
| Calls to visits | 1 in 3 | Under 1 in 5: rewrite the openers |
| Visits to quotes | 1 in 2 | Under 1 in 3: the demo isn't landing |
| Quotes to signed | 1 in 3 | Under 1 in 5: price or terms, ask why |
| Signed by end of February | 6 | Under 3: stop and review with 10 lost dealers' reasons |
| Onboarding hours | 12 or fewer | Over 15: cut setup scope before the next sale |
| Leads from the site, per dealer per month | 10 or more | Under 5: fix the site before selling more |
| Lost after go-live, first 90 days | 0 | Any: call them the same week |

Price rises follow the triggers in the pricing doc: 5 dealers live in one county, more than 40% of tier A calls closing, or Facebook leads shipping.

Under €200 a month: fuel for visits, one overnight in Galway a fortnight, printed one-page quotes. No paid ads: 112 named dealers don't need them.
